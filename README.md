# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes transactional retail data from the **Superstore Sales** dataset to uncover key factors driving sales performance, profit margins, regional growth, and customer segment behavior.

🔗 **View Live Notebook:** [Kaggle Superstore Analysis Notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/bhavanidoddamreddy/notebook5670589d20)

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered
* **Revenue & Profit Drivers:** Which product categories and sub-categories generate the highest profit margins versus top-line revenue?
* **Regional Performance:** How do sales volumes and profit performance vary across geographic regions?
* **Customer Segmentation:** Which customer segment (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office) delivers the highest customer lifetime value?
* **Shipping Impact:** How do different shipping modes impact order volume and overall order fulfillment timelines?

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills
* **Language:** Python / SQL (Pandas, SQLite)
* **Key Concepts:** Multi-table Joins, Aggregations (`GROUP BY`, `SUM`, `AVG`), Filtering (`WHERE`, `HAVING`), Window Functions, and Data Wrangling.

## 💡 Key Business Insights
* **Technology** generates the highest overall profit margin despite **Office Supplies** accounting for the highest total volume of orders.
* The **Consumer** segment accounts for over 50% of overall profit, making it the primary target for marketing campaigns.
* Certain sub-categories (like Tables) show high total revenue but negative profit margins due to high shipping costs and discounts.
